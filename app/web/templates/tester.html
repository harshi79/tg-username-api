Live requests against the production API. Base URL: https://tg-username-api.vercel.app/api/v1

Endpoint
GET /api/v1/check?username={username}

Accepts durov, @durov or https://t.me/durov. Press Enter to submit.

GET /api/v1/report?username={username}

Full report with characteristics and heuristic score. Press Enter to submit.

GET /api/v2/resolve?query={query}

Accepts usernames, numeric Telegram IDs, tg://openmessage?user_id=... deep links, and t.me URLs. Press Enter to submit.

POST /api/v1/check/bulk

Separate with spaces, commas or new lines. 0 / 15 usernames · Ctrl+Enter to submit

Bulk requests limited to 15 usernames.
